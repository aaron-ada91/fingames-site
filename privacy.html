Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE — à la validation finale]

This Privacy Policy explains what FinGames does — and does not do — with information about you.

It is short because the application collects nothing.

01THE SHORT VERSION

FinGames does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

There is no account, no login, no email address, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Everything the application knows about you stays on your device.

02WHAT IS STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The application saves the following locally, so that it can work between sessions:

  • your progress in the courses, and the best score of each validated step;
  • your practice history: dates, modes, difficulties, scores and ranks;
  • your preferences: language, theme, and reminder settings;
  • your subscription status, and whether the introductory screens have been seen.

This data never leaves your device. We have no server that could receive it, and no way to read it.

03WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

To be explicit, the application does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address;
  • your contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera;
  • your location, at any level of precision;
  • a device or advertising identifier;
  • usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural profiles.

No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking library is present in the application.

04PAYMENTS

Subscriptions are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play.

We never see, receive, or store your payment details. The store confirms to the application whether an active subscription exists, and nothing else.

The store applies its own privacy policy to the purchase, independently of this one.

05NOTIFICATIONS

The application can send you practice reminders, only if you enable them.

These notifications are scheduled and delivered on your device. No message passes through our servers, and none is sent when you have not asked for them.

You can turn them off at any time, in the application or in your device settings.

06CHILDREN

FinGames is intended for users aged 16 and over.

Since the application collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

07DELETING YOUR DATA

Deleting the application removes everything it stored on your device.

There is nothing to request from us, and nothing for us to erase, because we hold nothing.

Your subscription is held by the store and is managed there.

08CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future version of the application collects anything at all, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be made visible in the application.
