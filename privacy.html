Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 Lire en français

This Privacy Policy explains which data FinGames processes, where it is kept, who receives it, why, for how long, and what rights you have.

It distinguishes five situations: data that stays on your device, data sent to our subscription provider, data sent to our own server for the world ranking, duels and voice interviews, data processed by Apple for your purchases, and data that would only be sent if a component that is currently switched off were enabled.

01WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

The controller of the processing described in this policy is FinGames — Aaron ADA (EI), 67 avenue Pasteur, 91550 Paray-Vieille-Poste, France.

For any question about your data or to exercise your rights, write to fingames.support@gmail.com.

02THE SHORT VERSION

  • There is no account, no login, and no advertising, analytics or tracking library.
  • Your answers, progress and results are stored only on your device, with three exceptions described in section 5: the world ranking and duels, the voice interview and anonymous statistics on your first-launch answers.
  • World ranking: your pseudonym, avatar and experience points are sent to our server and shown to every FinGames player. You can delete your line from the Ranking page of the application, or ask us to.
  • Duels: when you create or accept a duel, its settings, your pseudonym, avatar and league, and your answers during the duel are sent to our server; duels are reserved for subscribers; your opponent sees your pseudonym, avatar, league, how many questions you have treated and, at the end, your score — never your answers.
  • Anonymous statistics: if you tick the box on the last first-launch question, your answers to those fixed-choice questions are counted, once, in aggregate totals. They are not linked to you and no individual answer is kept.
  • Voice interview: while an interview you start is running, your voice is sent through our server to Google (Gemini) to generate the interviewer’s spoken replies. The interviewer is an artificial intelligence and its voice is synthetic. Our server does not record it.
  • Our subscription provider RevenueCat receives technical and purchase data, including an anonymous identifier and a device identifier, in order to confirm whether your subscription is active. This data is processed in the United States.
  • Apple processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We never receive your payment details.
  • A crash reporter (Sentry) is included in the application but is currently switched off: it sends nothing.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

03DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The application stores the following on your device, so that it works from one session to the next:

  • the first name you choose to enter (it may be left empty) and the avatar you pick among the built-in illustrations — no photo is ever requested;
  • your answers to the first-launch questions: your situation, your goal, the level you declare, and how you heard about the application (anonymous totals of these answers may be sent to our server, section 5);
  • the placement test: whether you completed it, skipped it or left it, and the level it measured;
  • your progress: lessons and checks completed, best scores, experience points and league — your experience points are also sent to the world ranking (section 5);
  • your voice interview history: transcripts, durations and the weekly voice time used;
  • your practice and assessment history: dates, modes, desks and firms chosen, difficulties, scores, accuracy and response times;
  • your preferences: language, theme, sound, daily goal, and the time of your practice reminder;
  • your subscription status as last confirmed by the store, and whether introductory screens have been seen;
  • technical data kept by the subscription component (RevenueCat): the anonymous identifier described in section 4 and a cached copy of your subscription status and of the offers.

Apart from what section 5 describes, none of this data is sent to us. The placement test and your scores are calculated on your device and are used only to adapt what the application shows you; they produce no decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

Deleting the application deletes this data from your device, with one exception: the ranking identity (a random player identifier and its secret, section 5) is kept in your device’s Keychain, which iOS may keep after the application is deleted, so that you can find your ranking line again if you reinstall. Deleting the application does not delete anything kept on our server (sections 4 and 5).

04DATA SENT TO REVENUECAT (SUBSCRIPTIONS)

To know whether you have an active subscription, the application uses RevenueCat, a service provided by RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), which acts as our processor. The application contacts RevenueCat when it starts, when it returns to the foreground, when the subscription page loads the offers, when you buy, and when you restore a purchase.

RevenueCat then receives:

  • an anonymous identifier generated at random by the application on your device (it contains no information about you);
  • the identifier for vendor (IDFV) that iOS assigns to your device for applications of the same publisher;
  • your device model, the iOS version, the version of the application, and the languages set on your device;
  • the country of your App Store account, and technical indicators about the purchase environment;
  • the status of the iOS tracking permission (the application never asks for this permission and does not track you);
  • when you buy or restore a subscription: the transaction data provided by Apple — product purchased, price and currency, purchase and expiry dates, and transaction identifiers;
  • your IP address, which is technically transmitted with any connection to an online service.

RevenueCat does not receive your first name, avatar, answers, progress, scores or history. We do not send RevenueCat any identifier of our own, email address or name. Our server queries RevenueCat with your anonymous RevenueCat identifier, using a read-only key kept on the server, to confirm a subscription before a voice interview or a duel.

This happens whether or not you have subscribed: without it, the application could not know whether your subscription is active.

The device identifier (IDFV) is sent automatically by RevenueCat’s software; the application offers no setting to prevent it. We do not use it ourselves, and neither it nor any other data listed here is used for advertising or to track you.

Through RevenueCat’s service, we can consult the records it holds for our application — the anonymous identifier, the purchases and the technical data above — and statistics about our subscriptions, such as the number of subscribers, renewals, cancellations and revenue. None of this tells us who you are.

Although the identifier is anonymous, this data can relate to you indirectly and is therefore treated as personal data.

05DATA SENT TO OUR SERVER (RANKING, VOICE INTERVIEW, ANONYMOUS STATISTICS)

World ranking. So that players can compare themselves, the application sends our server: the pseudonym and avatar you chose, your experience points as they change (the kind of activity, the points earned or lost and the day they were earned; for a lesson only its identifier — our server decides what a lesson is worth, and also limits how fast points can be earned: a minimum delay separates two events of the same kind), and a random player identifier with a secret kept in your device’s keychain. No e-mail address, no real name is requested. Your pseudonym, avatar, experience points, league and position are shown to every FinGames player. Every player with a pseudonym appears in the ranking; you can delete your line from the Ranking page of the application (“Delete my ranking profile”), or ask us to; it is then erased from our server together with your duels, your XP journal and the reports made by you or about you. The deletion is refused while a duel is in progress. After a deletion, the application does not create a new line unless you join the ranking again; a new line starts at zero XP and the application then shows that zero (your lessons and history are kept). If you reinstall the application, your pseudonym, avatar and points are restored from our server. Because the pseudonym is public, you can report another player’s offensive name from the ranking page (the report, with a reference to that player, is sent to our server, which hides a name reported by several players) and hide any player for yourself.

Duels. A duel is a private challenge between two players, by invitation code. When you create one, our server records the invitation code (valid for 15 minutes), the world, the domain if you picked one, the difficulty and the duration you chose. When someone accepts it, the server records the two participants — shown to each other with their pseudonym, avatar and league — and gives each player a list of questions (their identifiers). During the duel, the application sends our server your answers (for each question: the option you chose, or that you skipped it) and the server notes when it received them; it uses them to compute the scores, decide the winner and award experience points, which only the server can do. Your opponent sees how many questions you have treated while you play, and the two scores at the end — never your answers. If a player gives up, or stops responding for more than fifteen seconds, the duel is lost for that player. If both players leave, both lose and nobody wins; losing a duel never removes experience points you already have. Our server keeps the result of a duel (the two scores, the outcome and the experience points awarded) for 30 days and the detailed answers for 7 days, then deletes them; an invitation nobody accepted is deleted at the latest one day after it expires. Experience points already awarded remain in your ranking total until you delete your ranking line. Deleting your ranking line also deletes your duels from our server. Duels are reserved for subscribers: before you create or accept a duel, our server checks through RevenueCat that a subscription is active; the identifier sent for this purpose is the one described in section 4. So that one subscription cannot unlock duels for many people, our server then keeps, linked to your player identifier, a one-way fingerprint of your subscription identifier (a truncated SHA-256 hash) and the date of the check; one subscription can unlock duels for at most three players over 30 days. You can report your opponent from the result screen: the report (your player identifier, the reference of the player reported, the reason, the date and the reference of the duel) is sent to our server and read by us to moderate the community.

Voice interview. The interviewer is an artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) and its voice is synthetic. When you start a voice interview, the audio captured by the microphone is streamed, only while the interview is running, to our server, which forwards it to Google (Gemini API) to generate the interviewer’s spoken replies and transcripts. So that the interviewer can greet you by name, the first word of the display name you entered in your profile is sent along with it, if you entered one; it is used for that interview only and is not stored by our server. The transcript is saved on your device only. Voice interviews are part of the subscription: before opening a session, the application sends our server the anonymous RevenueCat identifier described in section 4, and our server asks RevenueCat whether a subscription is active. Our server does not record the audio or the transcript; it keeps technical counters in a database (session start time and duration, and an identifier derived from your subscription) to apply the weekly voice time — identical after a reinstall or on another device — and prevent abuse.

Anonymous statistics. On the last first-launch question, a box, unticked by default and clearly worded, lets you share your answers anonymously; nothing is sent unless you tick it. If you do, the application sends once, to our server, only your answers to the fixed-choice questions (your situation, your goal, your declared level, how you heard about us) and the language you chose. It sends no identifier of any kind — no account, device or installation identifier, no ranking or subscription identifier — and no free text. Our server adds one to a set of counters (for example “students” and “students who heard about us through social networks”); it does not store the request or one person’s combination of answers. A small flag kept on your device only prevents the same answers from being counted twice. Because the counters hold no identifier, we cannot find, change or delete your answers afterwards.

Our server receives your IP address when the application connects to it; it is used to limit abuse and is held in memory for that purpose; it is not stored in the ranking or duel database nor with the counters. Our own technical logs record only a truncated one-way fingerprint (hash) of it, in two cases: when a voice interview starts or is refused (this includes a refused connection to the voice service), and when too many ranking registrations come from the same address. No other log line carries it. Our hosting provider’s infrastructure also keeps logs of the requests it receives, which include the IP address and the address requested (for a duel, this may include its invitation code).

07CRASH REPORTS

The application includes a crash reporter provided by Sentry. It is currently switched off and sends no data.

If we switch it on in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, stating where the reports are hosted and for how long they are kept. It would then only report failures: the error, where it occurred in the code, the device model and the system and application versions.

A crash report would never contain:

  • the first name you entered, or any text you typed;
  • your progress, your scores or your history;
  • which screens you visited, or how long you stayed.

The reporter is configured not to attach your IP address or any user identifier to a report. As with any connection, the IP address would still be transmitted to establish it.

08NOTIFICATIONS

If you enable practice reminders, iOS asks for your permission and the reminder is scheduled on your device. It passes through no server: the application does not use remote (push) notifications.

You can switch reminders off at any time, in the application or in your device settings.

09WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • no advertising, and no advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • no usage analytics, audience measurement or screen tracking: we do not measure what you do in the application — the only statistics we receive concern subscriptions (section 4) and, if you agree, anonymous totals of your first-launch answers (section 5);
  • no tracking across applications or websites, and no sale or rental of data;
  • no access to your contacts, photos, files, camera or location; the microphone is used only while a voice interview you started is running (section 5).

10PURPOSES AND LEGAL BASES

  • Checking, providing and restoring your subscription (RevenueCat, section 4): performance of the contract you enter into when you subscribe (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and, before you subscribe, our legitimate interest in knowing whether access must be granted (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Subscription statistics provided by RevenueCat (section 4), used to manage our activity: our legitimate interest in running and managing it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Answering your messages to support (section 14): our legitimate interest in answering you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), or the performance of the contract when your message concerns your subscription.
  • World ranking (section 5): our legitimate interest in offering a community ranking to players (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object at any time by deleting your ranking profile in the application or by writing to us.
  • Anonymous statistics (section 5): your consent, given by ticking the box (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR); the box is unticked by default and nothing is sent without it. Since no individual record is kept, no individual data remains to access or erase, and a consent given cannot be withdrawn retroactively on counters that hold no identifier.
  • Duels (section 5): performance of the contract, as the feature you start requires exchanging your answers with your opponent through our server (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and abuse, including the sharing of one subscription among many players, and in moderating reports (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Voice interview (section 5): performance of the contract, as the feature you start requires sending your voice to the AI service (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Crash reports, if they are switched on in the future (section 7): the legal basis will be stated in the updated policy.

Apart from what section 5 describes, the data kept on your device (section 3) is not transmitted to us: it is used on your device solely to make the application work.

The up-to-date list is published in RevenueCat’s data processing agreement:

Two other providers receive data in narrower cases, described in section 14: Google, which hosts the support mailbox and may process your messages in the United States under its own terms, and GitHub, which hosts our website.

The data described in section 5 is hosted by Railway Corporation (United States), our hosting provider, on servers located in the United States, and the audio of voice interviews is processed by Google LLC (United States) through the Gemini API. These transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards offered by these providers in their data processing terms.

12HOW LONG DATA IS KEPT

  • Data stored on your device: until you delete it or delete the application; the ranking identity kept in the Keychain (section 5) may remain on your device after the application is deleted.
  • Data processed by RevenueCat: for the duration of your subscription, then as long as needed to manage your access, restore your purchases and answer support requests. RevenueCat keeps it until we ask it to delete it or until our contract ends; we have not set an automatic expiry, and we delete a customer’s record when you ask us to (section 13). When our contract with RevenueCat ends, it deletes or returns this data on our request, under its data processing agreement.
  • Messages sent to support: deleted at the latest twenty-four months after our last exchange. We clean the mailbox by hand once a year and delete every conversation whose last message is more than twelve months old; the mailbox’s bin then keeps it for up to thirty days, and copies held by our email provider are governed by its own terms. A message that documents a rights request or a dispute may be kept longer, up to the legal limitation period (five years in principle), when needed to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.
  • World ranking: as long as your ranking line exists. You can delete it from the Ranking page of the application, or ask us to delete it. We do not delete inactive lines automatically.
  • Duels: the result of a duel (the two scores, the outcome and the experience points awarded) for 30 days and the detailed answers for 7 days, then deleted; an invitation nobody accepted is deleted at the latest one day after it expires; experience points already awarded stay in your ranking total as long as your ranking line exists.
  • Anonymous statistics: the aggregate counters are kept as long as we study our audience; they contain no individual record.
  • Voice interviews: our server keeps neither the audio nor the transcript; only technical counters (start time, duration, an identifier derived from your subscription) are kept in our server’s database for the rolling seven-day allowance and deleted after fourteen days (our server checks every hour); the same technical information also appears in our server logs, for abuse prevention.
  • Server logs: the technical logs of our server (event times, durations, and truncated one-way fingerprints of IP addresses and identifiers; never audio, transcripts, answers or names) and the request logs of our hosting provider, Railway (which include IP addresses and requested addresses), are kept by Railway for the period its service applies.
  • Duel anti-sharing fingerprint: as long as your ranking line exists; it is deleted with it. Reports: as long as the ranking line of the person who made the report, or of the person reported, exists.
  • Data processed by Apple: according to Apple’s privacy policy.

13YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and to data portability where it applies.

For the data kept on your device, you exercise these rights directly: you can change it in the application, and deleting the application erases it, except the ranking identity kept in your device’s Keychain (section 5). We hold no copy of it, except your world ranking line (section 5), which you can delete from the Ranking page of the application or by writing to us.

For the ranking, your duels and the reports: to have your ranking line deleted, write to fingames.support@gmail.com giving your pseudonym and, if you can, the approximate date you joined; we process the request ourselves and answer within one month. If there is a reasonable doubt about who you are, we may ask for one more piece of information, but we do not ask for an identity document as a rule; if several players could match, we will tell you. The deletion erases your line, your XP journal, your duels, your subscription fingerprint and the reports made by you or about you. It does not erase the request logs kept by our hosting provider (for the period it applies), RevenueCat’s records or Apple’s. You can also do it yourself from the Ranking page of the application.

For the data processed by RevenueCat, write to fingames.support@gmail.com. RevenueCat identifies you only by a random identifier, without your name or e-mail address, so we cannot tell which record is yours unless you give us something that points to it. If you bought a subscription, send us the order number shown on the receipt Apple e-mailed you: we will search for it in RevenueCat and, if we find your record, give you access to it or delete it. If we cannot find it, or if you never subscribed, we will tell you that we cannot identify you (Article 11 GDPR), and we may ask for one more piece of information. Deleting a record at RevenueCat does not cancel a subscription with Apple: you cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

We answer within one month, which may be extended in the cases provided for by the GDPR.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or with the authority of your country of residence:

CNIL
https://www.cnil.fr

14SUPPORT AND WEBSITE

If you write to fingames.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the content of your message, which are processed by our email provider, Google (Gmail), and used only to answer you.

Our website (https://fingames.app) is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States; GitHub B.V., Netherlands, for visitors in the European Economic Area), which receives the IP address of visitors when serving the pages, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. The website uses no cookies and no analytics; it only remembers the display theme you choose, in your browser.

15CHILDREN

FinGames is intended for users aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process data about children under that age.

We do not ask for your age or identity. Purchases are subject to the parental controls of your Apple Account (such as Ask to Buy).
